Disconnect from QuickBooks Online

The CMNV Operations Toolkit can be disconnected from CMNV's QuickBooks Online company at any time. Two equivalent paths:

From QuickBooks Online (Recommended)

The QBO administrator may revoke the toolkit's access from within QuickBooks Online itself, which immediately invalidates the toolkit's refresh token at Intuit's end:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as a CMNV administrator.
  2. Navigate to Settings (gear icon) → Apps → My Apps.
  3. Locate "CMNV Operations Toolkit" in the list of connected apps.
  4. Click Disconnect.

After this, the toolkit will no longer be able to read QBO data; any further attempt by the toolkit will receive an authentication error from Intuit.

From the operating workstation

The Treasurer can also clear local credentials by removing the stored refresh token from the toolkit's configuration file:

~/claude-sync/work/cmnv/tooling/qbo/intuit-mcp/.env

Specifically, deleting the QUICKBOOKS_REFRESH_TOKEN value disables the local copy. For complete revocation, perform the QuickBooks Online step above as well — that severs Intuit's record of the connection.

To reconnect after a disconnection, the Treasurer runs npm run auth in the toolkit's directory, which triggers a fresh OAuth handshake. See the Connect page for details.
