2. Purpose

The Software is a single-organization internal tool that connects to QuickBooks Online via the QuickBooks Online API to automate routine accounting, compliance, and tax-filing workflows for CMNV. These include preparation of California Form 592 nonresident-withholding filings, preparation of IRS Form 990 supporting workpapers, 1099-NEC reconciliation, and quarterly book reconciliation.
