Privacy Policy

CMNV Operations Toolkit
Effective Date: May 18, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how the CMNV Operations Toolkit (the "Software") handles data. The Software is an internal-use tool operated by the Treasurer of Chamber Music in Napa Valley ("CMNV"), a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. It is not offered to or used by any external party.

2. Data Accessed

When authorized via OAuth 2.0, the Software accesses CMNV's own QuickBooks Online ("QBO") company data through the QuickBooks Online API. This data may include:

  • Vendor and customer records (names, addresses, tax identification numbers stored by CMNV in QBO)
  • Transactions (bills, payments, invoices, deposits, transfers, journal entries)
  • Chart of accounts and account balances
  • Financial reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger, Vendor Expenses, and similar)

The Software requests only the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting OAuth scope. It does not request access to payments processing, user identity, profile, or contact information beyond what is necessary to operate against CMNV's QBO company.

3. How Data Is Used

QBO data is used exclusively to support CMNV's internal accounting and tax-compliance workflows, including:

  • Preparation of California Franchise Tax Board Form 592 (Nonresident Withholding Statement)
  • Preparation of IRS Form 990 supporting workpapers for CMNV's outside CPA firm
  • Annual 1099-NEC reconciliation
  • Quarterly book reconciliation between QBO and CMNV bank statements

4. Storage and Transmission

Data retrieved from QBO is processed and stored on a single CMNV-owned macOS computer. The Software does not transmit QBO data to any third party. Reports generated by the Software (e.g., completed tax filings) may be transmitted to:

  • The California Franchise Tax Board, via the MyFTB portal, for filings required by law
  • The U.S. Internal Revenue Service, via paper filing or authorized e-filing channels, for filings required by law
  • CMNV's outside CPA firm (currently Brotemarkle Davis & Co.), via that firm's secure document-exchange portal

OAuth tokens are stored locally in a configuration file on the operating computer; they are not embedded in the Software's source code, transmitted to any third party, or stored in the cloud.

5. Data Retention

QBO data accessed by the Software is retained only as long as needed to complete the workflow that requested it. Tax filings and supporting workpapers are retained per applicable tax-record retention rules (minimum 5 years for California withholding records, 7 years for IRS records).

6. Data Sharing

The Software does not sell, rent, or share QBO data with any party other than:

  • Intuit Inc. (from whom the data originates via the QuickBooks Online API)
  • Government tax authorities, only as required for legally mandated tax filings
  • CMNV's outside CPA firm, for the purpose of preparing CMNV's required annual filings

7. Security

The Software uses Intuit's official OAuth 2.0 implementation for authentication. Access tokens auto-rotate and are persisted only locally. The operating computer is administered by CMNV's Treasurer and is protected by macOS account-level authentication and full-disk encryption.

8. Your Rights as the QBO Account Holder

CMNV, as the owner of the connected QuickBooks Online company, may revoke the Software's access at any time via QuickBooks Online's Apps → My Apps page. Revocation immediately disables all further data access.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Software evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
